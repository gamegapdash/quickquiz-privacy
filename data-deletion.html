User Data Deletion Instructions

App: QuickQuiz (au.com.sourcecode.quickquiz)

Last updated: September 15, 2026

See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

You can delete your QuickQuiz account and associated personal data at any time. This page explains how, for Google Play / App Store data-deletion requirements and for users who cannot access the app.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (recommended)

QuickQuiz includes a Delete account feature on the Profile screen. This permanently deletes your account on our servers and signs you out on the device.

  1. Open QuickQuiz and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm as prompted:
    • If you signed up with email/password — enter your current password.
    • If you signed in with Google or Apple — enter your username to confirm.
  5. Submit the deletion request and wait for confirmation. You will return to the sign-in screen.

Note: This action cannot be undone. Quiz progress, streaks, XP, profile, avatar, and account information linked to that account will be permanently removed from our systems (subject to limited legal retention described below).

Guest players: If you never created an account, there is no server account to delete. Uninstalling the app removes local guest progress stored on your device.

What is deleted

When you delete your account (in-app or by email request), we delete or anonymize:

  • Your account credentials and profile (email, username, avatar);
  • Quiz attempts, scores, streaks, XP, and related progress on our servers;
  • Challenge / social links tied to your account where applicable;
  • Local session data on your device after deletion completes.

We may retain limited records when required by law (for example fraud prevention or dispute logs). We do not store payment card details. QuickQuiz does not currently sell in-app purchases.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app (lost device, login issues, or you deleted the app), email us:

support@quickquiz.net

Include in your message:

  • Subject line: QuickQuiz - Data Deletion Request
  • How you signed in (email/password, Google, or Apple)
  • Email address or username linked to the account (if known)
  • Confirmation that you want permanent deletion

We will verify your identity and process the request within 30 days.

Google Sign-In users

If you signed in with Google:

  • Use Option 1 in the app to delete your QuickQuiz account and server data, or
  • Optionally revoke app access in your Google Account → Security → Third-party access, and email us if you also want server-side data removed via Option 2.

Apple Sign-In users (iOS)

Use Option 1 in the iOS app, or email us (Option 2). You may also manage Sign in with Apple apps under Apple ID → Password & Security → Apps Using Apple ID.
